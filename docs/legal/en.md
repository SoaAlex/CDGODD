## 2. Terms of Use

### 2.1 Purpose and nature of the service

"Left or Right?" (the "Service") is a **humorous entertainment game**. Players
sort cards (objects, concepts, foods, public figures, brands…) by whether they
subjectively perceive them as leaning "left" or "right".

Displayed results **are not facts, not statements by the publisher, and not any
political, sociological or statistical truth.** They only reflect how anonymous
players swiped, for fun. No result should be taken seriously, or used to judge,
target, disparage or harass any person, brand or group.

## 4. Reporting and takedown policy

You can request that content be edited or removed — in particular if you are the
person depicted on a card, or if you hold rights (trademark, image, IP) or the
privacy interest concerned.

**Two channels:**

1. **In the app** — the "Report" button on any card. Past a certain number of
   reports, the card is automatically hidden and returns to moderation.
2. **By email** (recommended for a formal request by a rights holder or depicted
   person) — write to support@cestdegaucheoudedroite.com stating:
   - precise identification of the content (which card, screenshot or link);
   - the reason (infringement of image rights, privacy, a trademark, copyright,
     defamatory content, etc.);
   - your standing to act (depicted person, rights holder, or agent);
   - your contact details for our reply.

**Handling.** We acknowledge and review any admissible request promptly. Any
manifestly unlawful content is removed promptly upon actual knowledge. Removal
does not amount to an admission of liability.

## 5. Privacy

### 5.1 Players (anonymous by design)

The game is 100% anonymous: no account, no collection of identifying personal
data. Only ads may collect data, and only with your consent (disableable in
settings). An IP hash is used to fight abuse: kept 30 days, and cannot be
reversed to the original IP.

### 5.2 Persons depicted on cards

Where a card depicts a real person (name and/or image), that information is
personal data about them.

- **Purpose and legal basis.** Depiction for humorous, satirical and
  public-information purposes, under the publisher's legitimate interest and
  freedom of expression, without commercial exploitation of the person's image.
- **Political opinions.** The Service **does not create a political record** of
  any person: it attributes no real opinion to anyone. Results are aggregated,
  anonymous player perceptions, for fun — not a declared or verified attribute
  of the depicted person.
- **Rights.** Any person concerned has rights of access, rectification,
  objection and erasure. To exercise them, or to request removal of their
  depiction, write to support@cestdegaucheoudedroite.com (see §4).

### 5.3 Data contact

For any personal-data question: support@cestdegaucheoudedroite.com.
